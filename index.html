Le Répertoire
I. Spanish Cuisine from North to South and the Islands Gastronomy · A voyage in flavours

A leisurely journey down the Iberian table — from the pintxos bars of San Sebastián and the cider houses of Asturias, through the saffron fields of La Mancha, to the fried fish of Andalusia and the island kitchens of the Balearics and Canaries.

Along the way: why paella is a fighting word in Valencia, what the Moors left behind in the pantry, and how geography wrote Spain's menu long before its chefs did.

≈ 45 minutes With tastings on request Illustrated
II. Pesto de Pasta: the History and Stories of Italian Cuisine Gastronomy · La dolce vita, one plate at a time

Italy is less one cuisine than twenty regional love affairs. This talk winds from the basil terraces of Liguria to the ragù pots of Bologna and the lemon groves of the Amalfi coast, tracing how pasta conquered a peninsula — and then the world.

Expect nonna wisdom, a little opera, the truth about carbonara, and the story of how a humble Genoese sauce became a global obsession.

≈ 45 minutes With tastings on request Illustrated
III. The History of Norway from Mythology to Present Day History · Fjords, gods & modernity

From Odin and the world-tree to oil rigs and the world's happiest commuters — a sweeping tale of a nation carved by ice and sea. We sail with the Vikings, weather plague and union, and watch a poor fishing nation become one of the wealthiest countries on earth.

Featuring trolls, stave churches, polar explorers with questionable planning, and the quiet drama of Norwegian independence.

≈ 60 minutes Illustrated Suitable for all audiences
